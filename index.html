How to play

  1. The class picks one letter at a time.
  2. A right letter appears in the word.
  3. A wrong letter adds one line to the drawing.
  4. Think you know it? Say the whole word. The teacher taps Guess the word and types it in.
  5. A wrong guess adds one line to the drawing.
  6. Find the word before the drawing is finished to save him!

Your own words (optional)

One English word per line. Add the Chinese meaning after it to show at the end of the round, e.g. reunion 團聚
